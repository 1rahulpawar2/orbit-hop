Orbit Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Orbit Hop ("the app") is a simple offline arcade game. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location ourselves.

Data stored on your device

Your best score and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and information about how ads are viewed and tapped. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy settings" button on the main screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy "Remove Ads", the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is listed for a general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
